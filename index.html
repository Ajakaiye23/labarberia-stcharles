How a Visit Works · Cómo Funciona

El Proceso.

Walk In orCall Ahead.
No appointment needed. Just stop by at 327 Droste Rd during open hours — or call (314) 299-0816 to lock in a time. We speak English and Spanish.
Tell UsWhat You Want.
Bring a reference photo, describe it, or say "the usual." Maria or Bryan will confirm what you want before any clippers touch your head. Honest opinions if asked.
Sit, Relax,Get Sharp.
Real chair, real conversation. Cuts take 25-40 minutes done properly. Straight razor finish on every fade. Music or sports on the TV.
Walk OutLooking Fresh.
Hot towel finish, clean lines, looking sharp. Tip what feels right. We'll remember your style for next time — that's the difference.
